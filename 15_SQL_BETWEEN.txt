SQL BETWEEN
=======================================================

Database: HealtchareDatabse
Table(s): shown in each example

WHAT THIS MEANS
---------------
BETWEEN filters values inside an inclusive range. The starting and ending values are both included.

EXAMPLE 1 - Patients between ages 30 and 50
-------------------------------------------------------
REAL-TIME HOSPITAL QUESTION:
A hospital analyst asks: Show patients whose age is between 30 and 50.

SQL ANSWER:
SELECT
    PatientID, FirstName, LastName, AgeYears
FROM dbo.Patient
WHERE AgeYears BETWEEN 30 AND 50;

WHY THIS WORKS:
BETWEEN includes AgeYears values from 30 through 50, including both 30 and 50.

REAL OFFICE USE:
Useful for age-range reports and simple cohort selection.

EXAMPLE 2 - Patients born within a date range
-------------------------------------------------------
REAL-TIME HOSPITAL QUESTION:
A reporting analyst asks: Show patients born between January 1, 1980 and December 31, 1990.

SQL ANSWER:
SELECT
    PatientID, FirstName, LastName, DateOfBirth
FROM dbo.Patient
WHERE DateOfBirth BETWEEN '1980-01-01' AND '1990-12-31';

WHY THIS WORKS:
The query returns dates inside the range and includes both boundary dates.

REAL OFFICE USE:
Useful for date-range reporting and validating historical patient demographics.
